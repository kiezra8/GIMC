Who We Are

Medical Students.
Community Champions.

The Gomba Inspirational Medical Corps (GIMC) is a student-led association of medical students from Gomba District, Uganda. We believe healthcare is a right — not a privilege — and we are committed to making that belief a reality.

Our members, drawn from medical schools across Uganda, dedicate their time, skills, and passion to uplifting the communities they call home through organized outreaches, health education, and preventive care programs.

Our Foundation

Driven by Purpose.
Defined by Impact.

Three pillars guide everything we do — from the clinics we run to the lectures we deliver.

01

Service

We go where the need is greatest. Our outreaches bring free medical consultations, screenings, and treatments to communities that rarely see a doctor — fueled entirely by volunteer spirit.

Free consultations · Mobile clinics · Emergency response
02

Professionalism

We uphold the highest medical and ethical standards in everything we do. Our members are trained not just in medicine but in communication, leadership, and responsible practice.

Ethics · Continuous learning · Mentorship
03

Community Health

We address the root causes of poor health — poverty, lack of information, distance from facilities. Through education and advocacy, we empower communities to take charge of their wellbeing.

Prevention · Education · Advocacy

What We Do

Our Programs

Six pillars of community health — each designed to address a critical gap in Gomba's healthcare landscape.

Health Information Access

Distributing accurate, easy-to-understand health information in local languages through flyers, radio, and community dialogues.

  • Local language resources
  • Radio health programs
  • Digital health literacy

Preventive Health Services

Free screenings, immunizations, and preventive care clinics that catch disease early and keep communities healthy.

  • BP & diabetes screening
  • Immunization drives
  • Malaria prevention

Health Education

Interactive workshops and school programs that teach hygiene, nutrition, sexual health, and disease prevention from an early age.

  • School health talks
  • Nutrition workshops
  • Hygiene campaigns

Maternal & Child Health

Dedicated programs supporting expectant mothers and young children with antenatal care, safe delivery education, and postnatal follow-ups.

  • Antenatal clinics
  • Child nutrition support
  • Safe motherhood training

Community Support Programs

Connecting vulnerable families to social services, counseling, and resources — because health is more than medicine.

  • Mental health support
  • Social referral network
  • Youth empowerment

Student Leadership & Mentorship

Developing the next generation of Uganda's medical leaders through mentorship, leadership training, and academic support programs.

  • Peer mentorship
  • Medical school workshops
  • Leadership camps
